# Agency copy: SOCIALxBRAND PILOT

## Voice

Professional, creative, growth-driven. Confident and plain, never hype. Short declaratives.

- One idea per headline.
- **Serif-italic accent** on the single word that carries the meaning ("worth *showing*", "every", "*understanding*").
- Use the client's own lines where they exist (see `src/content/site.ts`). Don't paraphrase their promises.

## Honesty (non-negotiable)

- **Never write**: client names, results, metrics, ROI, growth percentages, follower counts, testimonials, awards, rankings, years in business, team size, certifications, partnerships, prices or a city.
- Work copy describes **only what is visible**: format, craft, setting, and the capabilities the piece demonstrates. "Kitchens, storefronts, flame and plating…" is fine. "+38% footfall" is not.
- Credit the production partner: "Production partner: Vishay Creations".
- Route suggestions are always framed as "a starting route; the real plan comes after we understand your business".

## Microcopy

- **CTAs** vary by context. Use "Start your Pilot Route" for primary actions, and "See the work", "Plan a route with this", "Explore every capability" as secondaries. Don't repeat the same label more than twice per page.
- **Errors** say what to do: "Add an email or a phone number so we can reply."
- **Hand-offs** are honest: "Opens WhatsApp with your brief written. Nothing is sent until you press send."

## Labels

Bracketed mono indices in the form `[ 04 ] Capabilities — 18 divisions`. Use the PDF's division names (full name on division pages, the short name in lists).

## SEO

Titles follow "<Page> · SOCIALxBRAND PILOT". Descriptions state what the page covers in plain words, with no superlatives. Positioning: "SOCIALxBRAND PILOT — Digital Marketing Agency — India & Global Markets".
