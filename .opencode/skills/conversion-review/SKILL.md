# Conversion review

**Progression to verify:** INTEREST → DISCOVERY → TRUST → DESIRE → ACTION.

## Clarity (10-second test)

- [ ] The hero says what SxBP is (a digital marketing agency) and why it's different (business-first, the right mix).
- [ ] "Not every business needs every service" is visible on home and on `/capabilities`.
- [ ] Every division page answers four questions: what it is, what it covers, what you get, and how the work runs.

## Trust (only from real evidence)

- [ ] The real work is visible within the first two scrolls (the hero fan and the screenings).
- [ ] Specifics are shown: deliverables, approach chains, the method.
- [ ] Real contact details are shown in the footer, on `/route` and in the JSON-LD.
- [ ] **There is no fabricated or implied proof anywhere.** Flag it immediately if found.

## Action

- [ ] The primary CTA ("Start your Pilot Route") is reachable from every page (nav pill, finale, page ends).
- [ ] The route builder runs: business → goal → route (with suggestions labelled) → details (with validation) → send (WhatsApp / email / copy).
- [ ] The nav badge reflects divisions added anywhere.
- [ ] "Or just talk to us" is always beside the builder.
- [ ] On mobile, primary actions are within thumb reach and tap targets are ≥ 44px.

## Friction

- [ ] No step demands more than it needs (only a name plus one contact method).
- [ ] Error messages are specific, and focus moves to the new step's heading.
- [ ] No dead ends: every screening and division page links onward.
