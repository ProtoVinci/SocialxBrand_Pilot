# Brand storytelling: SOCIALxBRAND PILOT

## Truths (from the client PDFs; never contradict or embellish them)

- An Indian digital marketing agency, serving India and global markets, industry-agnostic.
- It combines **Strategy + Creativity + Technology + Execution**, and rejects one-size-fits-all marketing.
- **"Not every business needs every service."** There are 18 divisions, combined around the business.
- Philosophy: **seen → remembered → trusted → chosen → moving forward**.
- Method: **Understand → Strategize → Create → Execute → Measure → Optimize → Grow**. "We start by understanding the business."
- AI amplifies creativity, accelerates strategy and helps people work better. It is one division, never the brand.
- Promise: **We will show, you will grow.** "We don't simply aim to create digital activity. We aim to create digital impact."

## Concept: the Pilot Route

Every business gets a route, not a package. The **red signal line** is the protagonist. It is drawn from the logomark, runs under the hero, tracks scroll in the nav, travels the method, maps each division's approach, connects the visitor's chosen divisions, and underlines "YOU WILL GROW". The BRAND PILOT stencil returns as **lane type** (striped numerals and fills).

## Story arc

| Beat | Emotion | Home act |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | interest | hero, with a fan of real reels |
| Tension | "activity isn't impact" | manifesto |
| Reveal | what marketing really is | ladder |
| Discovery | the work, the capabilities | screenings, capabilities |
| Progression | how they think | the route (method) |
| Trust | for whom, and how AI is used | audiences, AI |
| Payoff → action | the promise | finale, then the route builder |

## Rules for new beats

1. Name the beat's job in one line before designing it.
2. Use the motif (route line or lane type) only where it means something.
3. Prove with real assets (`src/content/work.ts`) and real specifics (`src/content/divisions.ts`). Never use adjectives in place of evidence.
4. End every page with a next step on the route.
